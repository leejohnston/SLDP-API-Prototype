Find Somerset Health and Care data you can trust

Access pseudonymised, multi-partner data through easy-to-use APIs. The first release focuses on people aged 65+ with frequent hospital use. You can explore aggregates for planning, build local analyses, and test ideas with synthetic data. No patient-identifiable data is published here. Any re-identification happens only by the GP for direct care and is fully audited.

Who this is for

  • Analysts and engineers in the NHS, Local Authorities and academia
  • Product owners checking feasibility, coverage and quality
  • Suppliers integrating Somerset-aligned open APIs

What you can do

  • Discover

    Search a single catalogue covering GP, acute, community and council data for an over-65 high-intensity cohort. Each API has examples and mock responses.

  • Test

    Run endpoints in your browser against synthetic data. No installation. See parameters, limits and example errors.

  • Build

    Build reports and models using clear governance, licences and source provenance. Consistent filters for time, geography and codes.

Popular APIs

  • Prototype

    Metadata Catalogue

    Lists datasets, schemas, refresh cadence and lineage. Builds trust and shows what exists.

    GET /v1/metadata/catalogue
  • Prototype

    Population Metrics

    Anonymous rates per 1,000 and readmissions by PCN/MSOA and month for planning and trend analysis.

    GET /v1/population/metrics
    Filters: period, geography, measure
  • Prototype

    Over-65 High-intensity Cohort

    Pseudonymised members meeting ≥N admissions in 12 months. Filter by PCN/MSOA for recall and review.

    GET /v1/cohorts/over65/high-intensity
    Filters: admissions_min, pcn, msoa
  • Prototype

    Unplanned Admission Risk

    Simple, explainable score using prior NELs, age band and recent LOS. For prioritisation tests.

    GET /v1/risk/unplanned-admission
    Params: cohort_id[]
  • Stable

    Opt-out Metrics

    Aggregate counts showing opt-outs honoured across sources for assurance to Caldicott/SIRO.

    GET /v1/governance/optouts
  • Open

    Reference Geographies

    Lookups and boundaries for PCN, LSOA and MSOA to ensure consistent small-area analysis.

    GET /v1/reference/geographies
